3Description

The TL-01 is an electronics and hardware engineer suitable for both hobby and career applications. The unit has been pulling things apart to figure out how they work since gen 1 and has yet to stop.

Anything ULV with digital or analog signal processing, low-power demands, or scaling-to-manufacture concerns is within the primary operating region. The unit has a tendancy to operate outside of the region whenever something piques interest.

The TL-01 is currently increasing its online footprint in order to share interesting tidbits and PCB design work, and to collaborate with others who share the interest. This document is part of that effort.
