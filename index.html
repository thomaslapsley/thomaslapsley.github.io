3Description

The TL-01 is an electronics and hardware engineer for whom electronics is both hobby and career. The unit has been pulling things apart to figure out how they work since it was young and has yet to stop.

Anything ULV with digital or analog signal processing, low-power demands, or scaling-to-manufacture concerns is within the primary operating region. The unit will also operate outside that region whenever something piques its interest.

The TL-01 is currently increasing its online footprint in order to publish interesting tidbits and PCB design work, and to collaborate with others who share the interest. This document is part of that effort.
